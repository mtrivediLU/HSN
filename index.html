If cancellations rise: what exactly counts as a same-day cancellation?

Defining a same-day cancellation

Same-day cancellation Draft, to confirm with the Surgical Program
Counts Scheduled elective cases cancelled on the day of surgery
Out of All scheduled elective cases for that day
Excluded Urgent and emergency add-ons; cases moved to another room and completed the same day
Edge cases to agree
Rebooked later the same day Patient did not arrive Bumped by an emergency Cancelled after entering the OR
Owner Surgical Program owns the definition. I build it, test it and document it.
The other three measures
Completed cases
First-case on-time start In room by scheduled start, tolerance to agree
Utilization of staffed OR time In-room minutes over staffed minutes, turnover rule to agree
Where HSN already reports a provincial definition, I use it rather than invent a new one.
