Step 03 · KPI definitions

Define a small, balanced KPI set

Every KPI needs an agreed meaning, denominator, owner, and use.

Proposed initial measure Decision it supports
Completed case volume, by service and urgency Is activity changing, and in which areas?
First-case on-time starts Where should the team investigate morning delays?
Same-day cancellation rate and reasons Which disruptions may be preventable?
OR utilization during staffed, available time How is usable capacity being used?
Turnover time Where is between-case flow changing?
Wait 2 and cases approaching target If in scope and reliably available
Where is access pressure building?
